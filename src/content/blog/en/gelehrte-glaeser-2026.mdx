"Science for everyone" is the motto of the Gelehrte Gläser, which translates roughly to "scholarly glasses". Instead of inviting people into a lecture hall, researchers go where people like to sit anyway, into the cafés and bars of Flensburg's city center. Anyone who feels like it can listen in over a drink and then talk to the researchers directly. The format is run by CIVU (Center for Interaction, Visualization and Usability), the Europa-Universität Flensburg, the Flensburg University of Applied Sciences and the Duale Hochschule Schleswig-Holstein. On 30 June 2026 the talks spread across the city center from 3:00 to 8:00 pm, two per venue.

## Smart irrigation at Lille Lykke

Green Ecolution was a guest at Lille Lykke on Norderstraße at 4:30 pm. I presented the project together with Prof. Dr. Simon Olberding, with whom Green Ecolution grew out of our master's research project. Under the title "Green Ecolution: Smart irrigation of urban green spaces" we showed what it is about: soil moisture sensors capture what happens below the surface, and the readings turn into irrigation plans and routes for everyday operations. The [test sensors](/blog/acht-von-zehn-sensoren) for it already sit in Flensburg's soil.

![Cedrik Hoffmann holds up a ground enclosure box and a Watermark sensor in front of the Green Ecolution roll-up while explaining them, with Simon Olberding standing to his right](../../../assets/blog/gelehrte-glaeser-2026-demo.jpg)

## A few streets away

The trees the talk was about stand just a few streets away. Afterwards there was time to keep talking right at the table, without a microphone and without a stage. That is what makes the format worth it.

Then we moved on ourselves. The other venues had talks from fields we never touch in the project, and they were worth the walk at least as much as our own contribution. In one evening and within a few hundred metres you hear what else the Flensburg universities are working on.

Formats like the Gelehrte Gläser create that closeness, so a warm thank you goes to the organizers.
